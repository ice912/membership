Notice of Privacy Practices & Patient Agreement

[Collection and Use of Protected Health Information (PHI)]
In accordance with HIPAA (45 CFR § 164.520), this service collects and maintains your Protected Health Information (PHI) to provide healthcare services.
- Collected Data Elements: Name/Alias, Email, Password, Date of Birth, Gender, Contact Information, and Protected Health Information (PHI) including medical history, prescription records, and health status.
- Purpose of Use (TPO): Your information is used for Treatment (identifying patients and providing care), Payment (insurance processing), and Healthcare Operations (customer support, quality improvement, and legal compliance).
- Data Retention and Destruction: PHI is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined or as required by State Board of Pharmacy record-keeping laws. Upon a valid request for account deletion, data will be permanently destroyed or de-identified unless legal retention is mandated.

[Individual Right of Access]
Under the HIPAA Privacy Rule, you have the following rights regarding your health information:
- Right to Inspect and Copy: You have the right to request an electronic or paper copy of your PHI. We will provide a copy or a summary of your health information, usually within 30 days of your request.
- Right to Amend: You may request a correction to your PHI if you believe it is incorrect or incomplete.
- Accounting of Disclosures: You may request a list (accounting) of the times we've shared your health information for up to six years prior to the date of your request.

[Security Standards and Data Protection]
To ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your PHI, we implement safeguards required by the HIPAA Security Rule:
- Technical Safeguards: All PHI is protected using industry-standard encryption (AES-256) both at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL). We utilize unique user IDs and automatic log-offs to prevent unauthorized access.
- Physical & Administrative Safeguards: Access to servers and data centers is strictly controlled. We conduct regular risk assessments and train all personnel on the secure handling of sensitive health data.

[Provision of Patient Medication Information (PMI)]
This service complies with FDA regulations (21 CFR Part 208) and State Board of Pharmacy counseling requirements by providing:
- Drug Name and Description: Proprietary and non-proprietary names and physical appearance.
- Dosage and Administration: Instructions on route, frequency, and duration.
- Indications for Use: The intended purpose and clinical effects.
- Safety Information: Potential side effects, contraindications, and drug-drug/drug-food interactions.
